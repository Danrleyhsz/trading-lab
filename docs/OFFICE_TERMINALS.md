# Immersive office panels

The approved panoramic scene and original character assets are unchanged. Agent navigation now shows original character cards, actual roles, backend status and backend room. No card schedules work or infers profitability.

Each occupied three-screen desk has separate transparent hit surfaces: the center opens the owner's recorded state/profile, the left opens the existing research/risk or market panel, and the right opens the general evidence journal. The two side views are laboratory views, not newly attributed private account data. The empty Trading Floor desk opens market/portfolio/audit without inventing an occupant.

Data Center uses its actual single monitor to open service/access diagnostics. The War Room's three displays open portfolio/account/audit views; market analysis uses four displays. Risk/meeting wall displays use existing controls/team views. Labels appear on hover or keyboard focus; navigation remains an alternative on small screens. These hit surfaces provide access to real existing information, not fake miniature monitor content. Full live projections on every decorative screen remain future incremental work.

Room names are small illuminated signs near the wall rather than large placards. Tooltips and focus glow provide discoverability. Opened monitor panels use a floating bezel, dimmed backdrop, constrained height, scrollable content, reduced-motion-aware opening animation and focus return to the clicked monitor. Original scene interactions are read-only; research/order control buttons remain explicit actions inside their respective panels.

The market tab adds larger spot and USD-M line charts from at most 90 session observations already received by the frontend. They are sampled price traces with observation-index spacing, not historical OHLC candles, trend forecasts or backtests. Empty sources wait for evidence; source failure labels the retained trace as session history. Equity charts continue using actual retained account samples; fund and evaluation capital remain separate.

Portfolio, research, history and access use a consistent trading laboratory terminal theme. Existing API/authentication, source identity, archived results, persistence, paper gates and AI configuration are unchanged. No dependency, new service, background activation or paid call is added.

Acceptance includes isolated TEST_ONLY interaction tests and browser review of the development preview (desktop/default viewport and 390×844 mobile). Preview state is independent of production and must never be imported into production. Runtime research/market work is not triggered for visual testing. Hosted visual acceptance is recorded separately after deployment.
